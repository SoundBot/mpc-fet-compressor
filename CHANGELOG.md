# Changelog

## 1.4.1 (first public release)

- Classic FET-style peak compressor: fixed threshold, Input/Output gain staging, 20 µs – 800 µs attack,
  50 – 1100 ms release, 4:1 / 8:1 / 12:1 / 20:1 and all-buttons mode.
- FET colour (even harmonics that grow with gain reduction), parallel Mix, sidechain high-pass (60/120/200 Hz).
- Analog-style VU meter on the touchscreen: gain reduction or output level.
- Hardware-style front panel with Q-Link mapping.
- A Default preset plus 25 presets for vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keys, synths and the mix bus,
  measured on material peaking at −6 dBFS.
- Choosing a preset updates the knobs on the MPC screen, with any SDK version.
